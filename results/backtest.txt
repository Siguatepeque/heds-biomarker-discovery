Headline result: using only papers published through 2024 (strictly before the first
hEDS GWAS meta-analysis appeared on medRxiv, 19 Sept 2025), the pipeline flags SLC39A13
as a candidate gene for hEDS/HSD via a purely indirect literature path.

Verified mechanism (see the diagnostic session that produced this file):
- No document in the pre-2025 corpus ever co-mentions the hEDS/HSD seed term and
  SLC39A13 in the same abstract (confirmed directly: `g.has_edge(seed, target)` is
  False for every cutoff tested).
- The only two pre-2025 papers mentioning SLC39A13 at all are from 2020 and 2021
  (PMID 32295219, Genes (Basel); PMID 31594391, Connect Tissue Res) - about
  spondylocheirodysplastic EDS, a separate rare recessive EDS subtype SLC39A13 was
  already known to cause.
- The bridge concept is "juvenile connective tissue diseases" (MeSH D003240). Its
  co-occurrence weight with the hEDS/HSD seed term was 1 through 2023 and 2024-dated
  papers (i.e. below the >=2 threshold), and crossed to 2 specifically in papers dated
  before 2025 - which is what flipped SLC39A13 from "present but not a candidate" to
  "candidate" between the 2024 and 2025 cutoffs.
- Independent confirmation: Petrucci-Nelson et al., "Complex Genetics and Regulatory
  Drivers of Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome," medRxiv 2025.09.19.25336146, found
  genome-wide-significant signal at SLC39A13 in a ~1,800-case/~5,000-control GWAS
  meta-analysis - published after this cutoff, using a completely different method
  (genotyping, not literature mining).

Honest limitation: SLC39A13 was not a total unknown - it's the established cause of a
different, rare EDS subtype, which is exactly why it had enough of an indirect
literature trail for this method to reach it. ACKR3, the GWAS's other major locus, has
essentially no connective-tissue literature footprint before 2025 (absent from the
corpus at every cutoff tested) and this method correctly finds nothing to say about it.
Literature-based discovery can get ahead of the field when a candidate has *any*
indirect textual trail to follow; it has nothing to offer for a genuinely de novo
finding with no prior textual trace.
