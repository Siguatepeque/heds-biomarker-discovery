Archived headline numbers under the original method, papers before 2022: 733
documents, 8 candidates, SLC39A13 at rank 8 with Adamic-Adar 0.860365.

What the cutoff block above records: using only papers dated through 2021,
the pipeline filed SLC39A13 as a candidate via an indirect literature path.
The GWAS meta-analysis that later reported signal at that locus is
Petrucci-Nelson et al., medRxiv 2025.09.19.25336146 (21 Sept 2025), 1,815
cases and 5,008 controls. That locus is multi-candidate and includes SLC39A13
and PSMC3. The GWAS reports an association. It does not establish causality.

This result went through two rounds of correction before it was kept. Both
are recorded because they describe the validation work:

ROUND 1 - found via an unrelated exploratory run: loosening the co-occurrence threshold
surfaced KLK15 (another 2024 hEDS gene) as a weak signal. Tracing it back led to PMID
40949095 (iScience, Aug 2025), titled "KLK15 alters connective tissues in hypermobile
Ehlers-Danlos syndrome" - i.e. a paper that directly, explicitly studies KLK15 in hEDS.
PubTator tags the gene correctly but normalizes the disease mention "hypermobile
Ehlers-Danlos syndrome" down to the generic Ehlers-Danlos syndrome MeSH ID (shared by
every subtype) rather than a hEDS-specific one. The pipeline's seed detection was
matching against the graph's single stored display name per entity ID - which is
whichever surface text happened to be recorded first, an arbitrary and unreliable
choice for a high-degree ID with many different real-world phrasings. Fix: seed
detection now scans each document's own raw annotation text directly
(discover_candidates.seed_documents), not the graph's aggregated per-ID name.

ROUND 2 - re-running the SLC39A13 backtest after that fix initially showed it as
"already directly studied" by 2022, which would have meant no prediction case at all.
Investigating why turned up two problems in the SEED_PATTERNS list:
  1. The pattern "ehlers-danlos syndrome type 3" (meant to catch the old pre-2017 name
     for hEDS) is a naive substring match, so it also matched "Ehlers-Danlos syndrome,
     spondylodysplastic form type 3" - a different, specific EDS subtype that SLC39A13
     is actually already known to cause. Fix: added "spondylodysplastic" /
     "spondylo-dysplastic" to EXCLUDE_PATTERNS.
  2. "generalized joint hypermobility" (GJH) was in SEED_PATTERNS, but GJH is a
     clinical sign, not a diagnosis - PMID 31594391 explicitly studies GJH as a
     population distinct from EDS ("crucial to distinguish the genetic basis of GJH
     from... EDS"). Fix: removed it from SEED_PATTERNS (kept in fetch_literature.QUERY,
     which only controls corpus scope, not seed status - a paper can legitimately
     belong in the corpus without counting as direct evidence of studying hEDS itself).

After both fixes, the check was: no document before 2022 co-mentions an hEDS/HSD
seed term and SLC39A13 in the same abstract under the alias list then in use
(confirmed by querying the graph directly), and neither of the two source papers
that discuss SLC39A13 (PMID 32295219, Genes (Basel) 2020; PMID 31594391, Connect
Tissue Res 2021) is itself a seed document. The connection was built from bridge
concepts shared between hEDS/HSD seed documents and these two papers, most
substantially "juvenile connective tissue diseases" (MeSH D003240, appearing in 54
hEDS/HSD seed documents, under the hub-degree cutoff, with a weight-2 edge to
SLC39A13), plus weaker single-mention contributions via tenascin-X, COL1A1, and
"type I procollagen." The Adamic-Adar score (0.86) is the sum of 1/log(degree)
over those bridges, hand-verified against the raw graph at the time.

Limitation note, revised: SLC39A13 was not new to connective-tissue literature
going in. It defines a different, rare, recessive EDS subtype
(spondylocheirodysplastic EDS), which is why an indirect trail existed to follow.
ACKR3 was absent before 2025 but appears in the cached GWAS abstract at the
2026 cutoff; it was not an indirect candidate. KLK15 also had direct paper
evidence in the corpus. Corpus coverage limits which genes this method can
retrieve. Neither absence from a shortlist nor presence on one establishes
a gene's biological role.

Status check against the full corpus (no cutoff), as run: SLC39A13 was still
filed as a candidate, not yet "already studied." Exactly one document in the
corpus directly co-mentions an hEDS/HSD seed term and SLC39A13 in the same
abstract - PMID 41001447, the GWAS preprint itself (medRxiv, 21 Sept 2025). The
2-document floor the snapshot applied to direct filing was therefore not cleared.
That single-paper lag is a threshold effect of the old rule. Direct filing
independent of min_weight is the corrected rule; under it, the preprint counts
as a direct co-mention on its own. The corrected full-corpus run excludes
SLC39A13, VWF, and C1R from the indirect list. The pre-2022 SLC39A13 result
remains, but it is still retrospective: development used knowledge of the
later GWAS, and no independent predictive benchmark has been completed.
